Investment Philosophy

Early disease detection.
Decentralised diagnostics.
Scalable clinical platforms.

Altiora Health Ventures is an operator-led initiative investing in European MedTech companies from Seed to Series B. Our founding partners have collectively built, led, and exited across Europe's diagnostics, medtech, and clinical infrastructure landscape.

Target investments are €0.5–3m at entry, with reserves for up to €6m per company and a target ownership of 10–25%. Our primary geography is Europe, with selective North American exposure where portfolio companies require it.


We do not intend to invest passively. We bring clinical validation expertise, regulatory strategy, commercial network access, and operational discipline to every board seat — the same capabilities that built SYNLAB into Europe's leading diagnostics network.

Fund I

Fund I — in formation

Altiora Health Ventures Fund I is being formed as a Luxembourg vehicle to institutionalize our operator-led investment approach. The fund has not yet held a first close and is not yet operational.

We are engaging prospective institutional LPs ahead of a planned first close. Full fund documentation, including the LPA, ILPA-aligned DDQ, and data room access, is available to qualified institutional investors following an initial conversation.

Final fund structure, regulatory status and AIFM arrangements remain subject to confirmation as formation proceeds.
